What is the From Unit?

from_unit_section_html

What is Square Meter? A Square Meter (square metre) is a unit of area defined as the area of a square with sides one meter long. It's a simple and widely understood metric measurement used for measuring land, rooms, and other spaces.

In India, Square Meter is commonly used nationwide across various real estate documents, including government land records, municipal plans, RERA (Real Estate Regulation and Development Act) filings, construction drawings, and banking files related to loans and mortgages. Properties in apartments, commercial complexes, and housing societies often quote area in Square Meters, especially in urban and metro markets.

The Square Meter is part of the metric system, introduced in India during modernization efforts to provide uniform measurements consistent with international standards. Though India historically had many local units, the metric system became the legal standard across states for official use, including real estate and construction.

Square Meters appear not only in real estate but also in agriculture for land measurement, in surveying for land records, as well as architecture and infrastructure planning projects across the country.

Area in Square Meters is generally calculated by multiplying length by breadth (both measured in meters), e.g., a 5-meter length and 4-meter breadth would make an area of 20 Square Meters.

What is the To Unit?

to_unit_section_html

What is Square Yard? A Square Yard is a unit of area representing the area of a square with each side one yard long. Since a yard measures three feet, Square Yard is widely used in India, especially in land measurement and real estate markets, alongside metric units.

Across India, Square Yard remains popular, particularly in many states and for larger residential plots, agricultural land, and commercial properties. It is common in contract negotiations, brokers' listings, and property registration paperwork. In metro cities like Mumbai and Pune, Square Yard is often used in property transactions to describe plot sizes and land area.

The Square Yard originates from the Imperial system, which was historically used during British rule in India and remains entrenched in traditional land measurement. Although metric units like Square Meter are official standards, Square Yard has retained its practical use, especially for measuring and selling land.

Square Yard is used by brokers, builders, investors, and landowners for property dealings, surveyors in measuring plots, and in documentation related to agriculture or urban development projects. It also appears in municipal and government records where older land surveys still recognize imperial units.

As with Square Meter, the area in Square Yards is calculated by multiplying the length by breadth measured in yards, making it easy to compute the area once the linear dimensions are known.

Technical Details

technical_details_html

Mathematically, 1 Square Meter is approximately equal to 1.19599 Square Yard. This factor arises because one meter equals about 1.09361 yards, and when converting square units, this length ratio is squared (1.09361 × 1.09361 ≈ 1.19599).

This means that each Square Meter covers a slightly larger area than one Square Yard.
