What is the From Unit?

from_unit_section_html

What is Square Foot? A Square Foot is a unit of area measurement representing a square shape with sides one foot (12 inches) long. Simply put, if you imagine a square measuring one foot on each side, the area inside it is one Square Foot.

In India, Square Foot is widely used in residential and commercial real estate. Property sizes for flats, shops, offices, and smaller plots are commonly described in Square Feet in government land records, RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority) filings, municipal property plans, and bank loan documents. It helps buyers and sellers easily communicate property dimensions in urban markets.

The Square Foot unit has its origins in the imperial measurement system introduced during British rule in India but remains popular due to its convenience for everyday use. While India officially uses metric units for many purposes, Square Foot continues as the default unit for property area across many Indian states.

Square Foot also appears in domains beyond real estate, including construction project planning, architecture blueprints, infrastructure layouts, and agricultural land surveys for smaller landholdings.

When dimensions for length and breadth are measured in feet, the area calculation is simple: Area (in Sq Ft) = Length (ft) × Breadth (ft). This formula is routinely applied by surveyors, builders, and architects.

What is the To Unit?

to_unit_section_html

What is Acre? An Acre is a larger unit of area traditionally used to measure land, especially plots, agricultural fields, or larger real estate parcels. One Acre represents a total area roughly equal to 43,560 Square Feet, making it suitable for describing expansive tracts of land.

Across India, Acre remains a vital unit in rural and semi-urban land measurement, agricultural holdings, and land registration processes. While metros and urban developments often use Square Feet for smaller properties, Acre provides a better scale for farms, development sites, and village plots. It is recognized widely in land records, survey maps, government land transactions, and loan or mortgage documents, particularly when dealing with sizeable land portions.

The Acre is a traditional Anglo-American unit stemming from historical land measurement practices, reaching into Indian land measurement systems through colonial influence. Unlike Square Foot, Acre is not metric but is globally familiar in land dealings, including in Indian real estate.

Acre is commonly applied in agriculture, real estate development, infrastructure projects, and surveying large tracts. It facilitates easy buying, selling, and legal processes for extensive estates or construction sites.

The area in Acres is derived by measuring length and breadth in feet or metres, then converting as: Area (Acres) = (Length × Breadth) / 43,560 when using feet, highlighting its basis in rectangular land measurement.
