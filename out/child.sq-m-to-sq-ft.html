Technical Background of Square Meter to Square Foot Conversion

Converting from square meters (m²) to square feet (ft²) involves multiplying the area value in square meters by the conversion factor 10.7639. This factor represents the exact number of square feet contained in one square meter, reflecting the difference between the metric and imperial systems of measurement.

Mathematically, the formula is: Area in ft² = Area in m² × 10.7639. For example, a property measuring 50 m² would be 50 × 10.7639 = 538.195 ft². This directional conversion is crucial in Indian real estate, where official documents and construction plans often use square meters, but buyers and agents prefer square feet for better spatial understanding.

Using this precise factor ensures accuracy in property valuation, price negotiation, and legal documentation across Pan-India markets. It helps avoid errors that could arise from approximate conversions or rounding, which might otherwise affect financial and legal outcomes. This conversion is especially relevant in cities like Mumbai, where real estate transactions demand clarity and precision in area measurement.

By consistently applying this conversion, stakeholders maintain uniformity and transparency, facilitating smoother communication and decision-making throughout the property buying and selling process.
